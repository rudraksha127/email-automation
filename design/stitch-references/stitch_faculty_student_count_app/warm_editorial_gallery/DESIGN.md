## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, museum-grade aesthetic crafted for digital creators, photographers, collectors, and design-conscious visual artists. The brand tone is quiet, discerning, and hospitable—allowing artwork and high-resolution media to remain the undisputed focal point.

Drawing from modern **Minimalism** blended with contemporary **Editorial Curation**, the system prioritizes expansive spatial rhythm, subtle tactile contrasts, and high-legibility typographic styling. The emotional atmosphere evokes a physical exhibition space: sunlit, peaceful, uncluttered, and meticulously framed.

Key design tenants:
- **Image-First Primacy:** Surfaces, containers, and borders step back to frame photography and illustrations without stylistic noise.
- **Warm Architectural Whites:** Pure synthetic whites are reserved exclusively for active input cards and isolated highlights, grounded against soft plaster and linen neutrals.
- **Harmonious Hybrid Typography:** Clean geometric sans-serif infrastructure paired with expressive editorial serif accents creates an intentional tension between modernity and classical curation.
- **Restrained Tactility:** Generous pill geometries, soft hairline borders, and weightless ambient drop shadows establish a warm, physical presence without heavy skeumorphic ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an organic warm spectrum, eliminating harsh contrast in favor of deep charcoals, soft linework, and calming stone neutrals.

### Surface Hierarchy & Backgrounds
- **Base Canvas (`#fbfaf8`):** The primary page backdrop. A warm, delicate off-white that reduces eye strain and provides an organic gallery-wall atmosphere.
- **Secondary Surface (`#f5f4f0`):** Applied to nested panels, inactive pills, sidebar navigation containers, and subtle component backgrounds.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#ffffff`):** Pure white used selectively for focused media cards, floating popovers, and interactive input fields.

### Content & Typography
- **Primary Ink (`#121212`):** Velvet off-black used for primary headings, prominent labels, hero text, and solid call-to-action buttons.
- **Secondary Ink (`#71716e`):** Muted stone charcoal for secondary copy, metadata, timestamps, and secondary icon glyphs.
- **Subtle Ink (`#9e9c96`):** Low-contrast gray for placeholders, tertiary labels, and auxiliary caption notes.

### Structural Lines & Borders
- **Border Subtle (`#e8e6e1`):** Gentle structural hairlines separating content sections and defining unselected chips.
- **Border Default (`#dfdcd5`):** Standard 1px outline for cards, form fields, and floating control buttons.

## Typography

The typographical pairing relies on the functional precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for systematic UI elements, structural headings, and readable body text, balanced with **Newsreader** for artistic editorial statements.

### Editorial Headline Accent
In headline compositions such as "A space for *creativity*", the primary word is set in Newsreader Regular Italic to generate authentic art-book elegance alongside the contemporary geometric sans.

### Sizing and Hierarchy
- Display hero typography establishes quiet authority in hero showcases.
- Body text maintains ample line height (1.5x–1.6x) for effortless scanning through artist statements and technical metadata.
- Numerical metadata (resolution, dimensions, dates) adopts `body-sm` with tabular lining figures to ensure tidy vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column layout on desktop that transitions gracefully to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

### Grid & Canvas Rhythm
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Max container width capped at 1440px with `margin-desktop` (3rem) horizontal boundary padding and 1.75rem gutters between imagery tiles.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** `margin-tablet` (2rem) boundary margin with 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column hero with dual-column stagger or masonry grid for artwork discovery, utilizing a tight 1rem outer canvas margin.

### Vertical Spacing & Flow
Whitespace acts as a functional framing mechanism. Sections are separated by `space-2xl` (3.5rem) to ensure artworks do not compete visually with adjacent categories, search headers, or collection titles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via delicate tonal shifts, hairline structural boundaries, and feathered ambient shadows rather than stark elevation planes.

### Ambient Shadow Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Surfaces):** Media cards within masonry layouts and baseline panels rely exclusively on a 1px border (`#dfdcd5` at 80% opacity) against the warm neutral background.
- **Level 1 (Hover & Card Lift):** Active hover states on art tiles and standard dropdown containers apply:
  `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 26, 22, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 26, 22, 0.03)`
- **Level 2 (Floating Overlays & Dialogs):** Modal dialogs, upload drawers, and persistent mobile pill bars apply:
  `0px 16px 36px -8px rgba(28, 26, 22, 0.09), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 26, 22, 0.04)`

### Glassmorphism & Translucency
Navigation headers and floating control pills utilize a glassmorphic background blur (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(251, 250, 248, 0.85)`), allowing the colors of scrolling artwork to gently diffuse underneath.

## Shapes

The shape system blends generous structural corner radii with organic pill profiles, reinforcing an approachable yet curated physical museum identity.

- **Artwork Containers & Media Cards:** Consistently use `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) to soften the perimeter of photography and renders without clipping critical edge content.
- **Interactive Controls & Category Chips:** Use full pill geometries (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for search bars, navigation tabs, buttons, and filter chips.
- **Form Inputs & Inner Panels:** Use `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) for structured input fields, upload drop areas, and collection cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill Button):** Solid `#121212` background, pure `#ffffff` text, full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded with 0.625rem vertically and 1.25rem horizontally. Features a small leading icon (16px) with 8px gap.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent or `#ffffff` surface, 1px border (`#dfdcd5`), `#121212` text, full pill radius. On hover, background shifts to `#f5f4f0`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `#71716e` text, subtle hover shift to `#121212`.

### Category & Filter Chips
- **Active Filter Chip:** Solid `#121212` fill with white text, `rounded-full`, 0.375rem vertical padding, 0.875rem horizontal padding, text set to `label-md`.
- **Default Filter Chip:** `#f5f4f0` surface, subtle `#dfdcd5` 1px border, `#71716e` label. On hover, transition text to `#121212` and border to `#c8c5be`.

### Cards & Media Displays
- **Artwork Grid Card:** `rounded-2xl`, overflow hidden, 1px border (`#e8e6e1`), ambient hover lift. Media scales smoothly (`scale 1.02` over 300ms cubic-bezier).
- **Collection Folder Card:** Card consists of an upper rounded thumbnail stack or composite preview and a bottom metadata strip displaying folder name in `headline-sm` with item count below in `body-sm` (`#71716e`). Includes a trailing menu trigger (`...`).

### Form Elements & Search Input
- **Global Search Bar:** Full pill shape, `#ffffff` surface, subtle 1px border (`#dfdcd5`), leading search glyph in `#9e9c96`, input text in `body-md`. Focus state uses an outline in `#121212` with 2px offset.
- **Upload Drop Zone:** Generous `rounded-2xl` container with a 1.5px dashed border (`#c8c5be`), `#fbfaf8` background, centered upload glyph, and helper copy indicating supported MIME formats and maximum file sizes.
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** `rounded-xl`, 1px border (`#dfdcd5`), `#ffffff` background, `body-md` typography, 0.75rem vertical and 1rem horizontal inner padding.

### Navigation
- **Desktop Navigation:** Top bar anchored by the brand wordmark (`headline-sm`, font-weight 600), inline horizontal text links with a sliding pill/underline indicator, centered or right-aligned search, and trailing utility controls (Theme, Notifications, Profile Avatar).
- **Mobile Floating Bar:** Fixed bottom pill nav container floating 1rem above the viewport bottom, featuring an integrated elevated center button (`+`) for creation workflows.
